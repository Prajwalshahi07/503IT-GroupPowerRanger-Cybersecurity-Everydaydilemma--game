9. Task 5 - Create an Appendix Evidence Map 

Why appendices matter 

 

Your individual portfolio should not be overloaded with screenshots and raw evidence in the main body. Appendices help you organise evidence such as meeting minutes, screenshots, canvases, storyboards, prototype evidence, feedback and contribution logs. However, you must refer to the appendices in the main report.
