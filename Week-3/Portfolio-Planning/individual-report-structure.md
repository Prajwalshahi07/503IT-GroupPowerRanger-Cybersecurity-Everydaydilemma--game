Cover Page
Include module title, assignment title, project/game title, student ID, author name if allowed, group number, tutor, date, and word count. If the template asks for an abstract, include a short 80-120 word summary.
Contents Page
List all main sections and appendices. Keep page numbers updated if possible. Do not overcomplicate it.
1. Introduction
Briefly introduce the 503IT group project, the educational cyber-safety game, your target audience, your group role, and what the portfolio will demonstrate.
2. Project Overview and My Role
Explain the group game concept, chosen tool, target audience, cyber-safety learning objective, and your specific responsibilities. Make clear what you personally owned or contributed to.
3. Development and Individual Contribution
This is the core section. Explain your personal contribution to design, research, UX, prototype development, GitHub evidence, testing, documentation, or presentation planning. Refer to screenshots, commits, minutes, and design/prototype evidence in appendices.
4. Testing, Feedback and Iteration
Explain how the game/prototype was tested, what feedback was received, what problems were found, and what improvements were made. Link to testing evidence, peer review, bug-fix logs, screenshots, and iteration logs.
5. Teamwork and Communication
Explain how the group communicated, divided tasks, handled leadership, used meetings, managed deadlines, and solved conflicts or gaps in contribution. Use meeting minutes and task logs as evidence.
6. Reflection on Professional Development
Use Gibbs or Kolb. Reflect on what happened, what you felt or observed, what went well, what did not go well, what you learned, and what you would improve in future group projects.
7. Ethical and Responsible AI Use
Explain whether and how AI was used. Mention brainstorming, grammar checking, idea refinement, code support, or debugging only if true. State how you checked, edited, and took responsibility for the final work.
8. Conclusion
Summarise your contribution, learning, professional skills gained, and next development priorities.
References
List external sources in APA7 format. Include sources for cyber safety information, UX/accessibility information, game design examples, and tools if referenced.
Appendices
Number appendices clearly and link them in the main body. Include meeting minutes, screenshots, GitHub commits, canvas, storyboard, testing results, iteration logs, peer/tutor feedback, AI use log, and presentation drafts.
