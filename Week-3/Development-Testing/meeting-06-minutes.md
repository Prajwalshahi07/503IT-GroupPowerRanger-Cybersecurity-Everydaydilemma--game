## Agenda Items
The primary objective of this meeting was to update and refine the scenarios for the **PLAY** part of the game. The three selected student scenarios discussed are:
1.  **Urgent Assignment Re-upload Email:** A high-pressure scenario targeting student anxiety around deadlines.
2.  **Fake IT Support Password Reset:** A technical spoofing scenario impersonating institutional IT services.
3.  **Bank Security Alert Text:** A smishing (SMS phishing) scenario simulating urgent financial fraud alerts.

---

## Decisions Made
* **Gameplay Structure:** Agreed that the "PLAY" section will load a 3-stage branching narrative engine utilizing the three selected student scenarios listed in the agenda.
* **Technology Architecture:** Decided to implement image-based storytelling to visually display the game concepts, narrative choices, and outcomes. Feedback mechanisms were established to define exactly how consequences and educational takeaways will be displayed to the player.
* **Scoring Mechanics:** Approved a unified point system to track player performance:
    * `+25 points` for selecting safe/correct actions.
    * `-5 points` for selecting risky/compromised choices.
